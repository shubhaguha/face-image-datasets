Shared provenance of face datasets

Each line shows where a dataset's images came from. 21 of the 35 entries take their images from another dataset, and LFW alone has 20 known derivatives, shown here as 5 individual datasets and 2 groups. Six datasets have been withdrawn (dashed borders), and derivatives of four of those are still distributed. RFW, BFW and LAOFIW take only their names from LFW: none of them contains LFW images.
